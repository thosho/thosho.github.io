Privacy Policy for Totag : AI Bookmark Manager

Browser Extension

1. Introduction

Thosho Tech built the Totag Extension as a free tool. This SERVICE is provided by Thosho Tech at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The Totag extension is designed with absolute privacy in mind. When you click "Save Bookmark," the extension accesses the URL and Title of your currently active tab (Web History). This data is collected strictly for the purpose of creating a bookmark.

3. Data Storage (Your Data Stays With You)

We do not own, operate, or maintain any database servers for this extension. The extension authenticates securely using the Google Identity API and saves your bookmarks directly into a file (totag_backup.json) located inside your own personal Google Drive. Thosho Tech does not collect, transmit, view, or store your bookmarks or browsing history on any external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The extension interacts with Google Drive APIs to sync your data. Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Data

Because we do not collect your data on our servers, we have nothing to sell. Your data is 100% yours. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or browsing history to third parties for any purpose.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@thoshotech.com.
