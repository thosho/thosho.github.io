Privacy Policy for Quaiz

Effective Date: November 1, 2025 | Last Updated: November 1, 2025

Introduction

Thosho Tech ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Quaiz mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide:

  • User Name: Optional display name you provide for personalization within the App.
  • Quiz Data: Custom quizzes you create, quiz scores, and quiz history.
  • Preferences: App settings and user preferences stored locally on your device.

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, quiz performance statistics.
  • Advertising ID: For serving personalized ads (if not using Pro version).

Information from Third-Party Services:

  • We use Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect device identifiers and usage data.
  • Google Play Billing processes payment information for Pro upgrades (we do not store payment details).

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain App functionality.
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., displaying your chosen name).
  • Store your quiz history and scores locally on your device.
  • Display advertisements (free version only).
  • Process Pro upgrade purchases through Google Play.
  • Improve App performance and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage:

  • User names, quiz data, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and Room Database.
  • This data is not transmitted to our servers and remains on your device.

Third-Party Services:

  • Google AdMob: Collects advertising data (see Google's Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Billing: Processes payments securely (we do not access payment details).

Security:

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

Google AdMob (Advertising):

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Collects: Device identifiers, usage data, location (approximate).
  • Purpose: Serving personalized advertisements.

Google Play Services (Billing):

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Purpose: Processing in-app purchases for Pro upgrades.

These services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their practices.

Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your data stored in the App (Settings > About).
  • Delete: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
  • Opt-Out of Ads: Upgrade to Pro version for ad-free experience.
  • Update: Modify your user name and preferences at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

Permissions

The App requires the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: To load quiz databases and advertisements.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To attach images to quiz questions (optional, requested only when needed).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: To process Pro upgrade purchases.

Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you delete the App or clear app data.
  • Pro Purchase Status: Stored permanently in your Google Play account.
  • Ad Data: Retained by Google AdMob per their privacy policy.

International Users

Our App is available worldwide. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of information to countries outside your country of residence, which may have different data protection rules.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

  • Developer: Thosho Tech
  • Email: contact@thoshotech.com
  • Website: https://thoshotech.com

Consent

By using our App, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
