Privacy Policy for To Sched

Effective Date: September 6, 2025

Thank you for using To Sched, a film production scheduling application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our App. This policy is provided by Thosho Tech ("we," "us," or "our").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Our App is designed with your privacy as a top priority. We collect information in two ways:

a) Information You Provide Directly

All the project information you enter into To Sched is created and managed by you. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Project Information (Production Name, Director, Contact Details)
  • Sequence and Schedule Break Titles
  • Scene Details (Scene Number, Description, Scene Setting, Day/Night, Shoot Location, Cast, Equipment, Notes, etc.)

Crucially, all of this information is stored locally on your device's storage (localStorage). It is never transmitted, uploaded, or saved to our servers. We do not have access to your project data.

b) Information Collected Automatically (Third-Party Services)

To support the App, we use Google AdMob for advertising. Google AdMob is a third-party service that may collect information to provide personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device and network information
  • Ad interaction data and viewing data

We do not have direct access to the specific data collected by Google for ad personalization. For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms page.

How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide App Functionality: We use the information you provide (your project data) solely to power the features of the App. This includes saving your schedule, displaying it in lists, filtering it, and exporting it to Excel or image formats at your request.
  • To Support the App: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, which helps keep the App free.

Data Storage and Security

Your project data is stored exclusively on your device. This means:

  • You are in control. We cannot access, view, or share your projects.
  • Data is persistent on your device. Your data will remain on your device until you either uninstall the App or clear the App's data through your device's settings.
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all your project data from that device.

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of the application, but as the data is stored on your personal device, you are also responsible for its security.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal project data with any third parties because we do not collect it.

You, the user, can choose to share your data through the App's features:

  • "Export to Excel": This creates a local file on your device which you can then share at your discretion.
  • "Share Scene Card": This uses your device's native sharing functionality to share an image of a scene's details.

Data collected by Google AdMob is handled according to their privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
