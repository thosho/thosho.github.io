Privacy Policy for Wraiter

Effective Date: October 4, 2025

Thank you for using Wraiter, an application developed by Thosho Tech ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). Your privacy is critically important to us.

Our core principle is to be privacy-first. The text you write is yours, and we aim to collect as little data as possible.

Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide Directly:

  • Your Written Content: All text, notes, and documents you create in the editor are stored locally on your device's private storage. We do not have access to, collect, or store your written content on our servers.
  • Third-Party API Keys: If you choose to connect to external AI services (like Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, etc.), you must provide your own API keys. These keys are stored securely and locally on your device in the app's private preferences. We never transmit these keys to our servers.

b) Information Collected Automatically:

  • Advertising Data: To support the free version of the App, we use Google AdMob, which may collect and use data to provide personalized advertising. This may include your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and other technical information. You can opt out of this by purchasing the "Remove Ads" feature.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous, aggregated data about how you interact with the App's features (e.g., which AI tools are used most often, crash reports). This data is not personally identifiable and is used solely to improve the App's performance and user experience.

c) Information from Third Parties:

  • Purchase Information: If you purchase "Wraiter Pro" to remove ads, the transaction is handled by the Google Play Store. We receive a confirmation token from Google to verify your purchase, but we do not collect or have access to your personal payment information, such as your credit card number.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we handle for the following purposes:

  • To Provide App Functionality: Your written content and API keys are used locally on your device to enable the core features of the App.
  • To Process AI Requests: When you use an AI feature connected to a third-party service, your written content (the prompt) and your corresponding API key are sent directly from your device to that service (e.g., to OpenAI's servers). We act only as a facilitator; your data is not routed through our servers.
  • To Display Advertisements: To provide a free version of the App, we use Google AdMob to display ads.
  • To Process Payments: To handle the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase through the Google Play Store.
  • To Improve the App: To analyze anonymous usage data and fix bugs.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Third-Party AI Providers: When you use a cloud-based AI feature, the text you select and your API key are sent directly to that provider. Your interaction with them is governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • With Advertising Partners: We share information with Google AdMob to display ads in the free version of the App. You can learn more about how Google uses data in their privacy policy.
  • With Payment Processors: We use Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Local AI Model: When you use the on-device .task model (e.g., Gemma), all processing happens entirely on your device. No text content or data is sent to any server.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All sensitive data, such as your API keys and your "Pro" status, are stored in the app's secure, private storage on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
