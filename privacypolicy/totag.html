Privacy Policy for Totag

Effective Date: January 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Totag, designed and developed by Thosho Tech. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application. Totag is built as a "local-first" app, meaning we prioritize keeping your personal data stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

  • User-Provided Content: We store the URLs (bookmarks), titles, descriptions, tags, and folder names you create. This data is stored locally on your device in a private database.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic information about your device (model, operating system version) to ensure the app functions correctly and to assist with debugging.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized usage data regarding interactions within the app to improve overall performance and user experience.

3. Google Drive Integration & User Data

Totag offers an optional feature allowing you to back up and restore your bookmark data using your personal Google Drive account.

  • Authentication: If you choose to use this feature, Totag authenticates directly with your Google account using Google Sign-In.
  • Data Access & Scope: Totag requests access only to the specific files created by the app (drive.file scope). Specifically, it manages a single backup file (totag_backup.json). Totag cannot read, view, edit, or delete any other personal files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.
  • Data Usage: We use this access strictly to upload your backup file to your Google Drive and download it when you initiate a restore operation.
  • Data Sharing & Storage: Your Google user data and Drive contents are never sent to our servers, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising purposes. All transfers occur directly between your device and Google's servers.
  • Policy Compliance: Totag's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. AI Summarization & Data Handling

Totag offers AI features to summarize web content. Your data is handled differently based on the engine you select:

  • Engaine (Local AI): If you use Engaine, the text processing is performed entirely on your device. No data leaves your phone.
  • Gemini (Google) & Groq (Cloud AI): If you opt to use cloud providers, the text of the article you choose to summarize is sent to their respective APIs. This data is transmitted securely and is processed according to the privacy policies of Google and Groq.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the app.

  • AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • For more information on how Google handles ad data, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Permissions Requested

Totag requires the following Android permissions to function properly:

  • Internet Access: To fetch website titles, preview thumbnails, communicate with Cloud AI services, and execute Google Drive backups.
  • Access Network State: To verify if an active internet connection is available before attempting cloud actions.
  • Local AI Access (Engaine): To communicate with the local Engaine engine running on your device for offline summarization.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All your bookmarks, tags, and settings stay on your device unless you manually clear your app data, delete specific items, or uninstall the app. You can delete your Google Drive backup file at any time directly through the app or by deleting the totag_backup.json file from your Google Drive. We do not operate central servers that retain your bookmark database.

8. Children's Privacy

Totag is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
