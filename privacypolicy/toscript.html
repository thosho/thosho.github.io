Privacy Policy for Toscript

Effective Date: August 27, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Thosho Tech handles information in connection with your use of the "Toscript" mobile application (the "App").

Your privacy is extremely important to us. Our guiding principle is to collect the minimum amount of information required to provide you with a great product and service.

Core Principle: Your Scripts/Screenplays are Your Own

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have any access to the content of the screenplays you create in the ToscripT app. All scripts and text you generate are stored exclusively on your device's local storage. You have full control over your files.

Information We Collect

While we do not collect personal information directly, the App utilizes third-party services that may collect information.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

We use third-party services in our App to provide advertising and handle in-app purchases. These services may collect information used to identify you. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the App:

Google Play Services (for In-App Purchases):

When you choose to upgrade to the Pro (ad-free) version of the App, the transaction is processed by the Google Play Store. We do not collect or see any of your payment information. This process is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy

Google AdMob (for Advertisements):

The free version of ToscripT uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data about you to provide personalized ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information, and rough location data. You can learn more about how Google collects and uses this information, and how to opt out, by visiting their policies.

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

How We Use Information

To be clear, we do not use any personal information ourselves. The data collected by the third-party services mentioned above is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the service (e.g., processing your "Go Pro" purchase).
  • To display advertisements to support the free version of the App.

App Permissions

The ToscripT app requires the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Internet Access: Required to display advertisements in the free version and to load necessary web libraries.
  • File Access: The app uses the native Android file picker to allow you to open and save your script files. The app only has access to the specific files you choose to open or save.

Data Security

We value your trust. As all your script data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data is primarily in your hands. We recommend using your device's security features (like screen lock and encryption) to protect your files.

Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
