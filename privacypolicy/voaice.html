Privacy Policy for Voaice

Last Updated: July 28, 2026 (Version: 4.0)

Thosho Tech ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Voaice mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using Voaice, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Voice recordings and audio files you create within the App
  • Text notes and content you input
  • Language preferences and app settings
  • AI API keys (if you choose to provide your own)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics (features used, crash reports)
  • AdMob advertising data (for displaying ads)
  • Anonymous analytics data

1.3 Permissions Required

  • Microphone: For voice recording and speech-to-text features
  • Storage: To save and access your notes and audio files
  • Internet: For AI features, ads, and app updates
  • Notifications: To alert you about background processes

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing core app functionality (voice recording, TTS, STT)
  • Processing AI features (proofreading, translation, summarization)
  • Displaying relevant advertisements (via Google AdMob)
  • Improving app performance and user experience
  • Debugging and fixing technical issues
  • Sending important app updates and notifications

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

3.1 Local Storage

  • All your notes, recordings, and personal data are stored LOCALLY on your device
  • We do NOT store your notes or recordings on our servers
  • Your data remains on your device unless you explicitly share it

3.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob (for advertisements)
  • AI API providers (OpenAI, Google, Groq) - only when you use AI features
  • Google Play Services (for app functionality)

3.3 Security Measures

  • Data encryption during transmission
  • Secure API communication
  • No unauthorized access to your device storage
  • Regular security updates

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

4.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Ad interaction data

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.2 AI Services (Optional)

When you use AI features (proofreading, translation):

  • Text is sent to AI providers (OpenAI, Google, Groq)
  • Processed for the requested function only
  • Not stored permanently by us
  • Subject to their privacy policies

4.3 Google Play Services

For app distribution, updates, and in-app purchases.

5. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We DO NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share data only in these circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and safety
  • With service providers (AdMob, AI APIs) as necessary for functionality

6. YOUR DATA RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (all stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (uninstall the app or clear app data)
  • Export your notes (using app's export feature)
  • Opt-out of ads (by purchasing "Remove Ads")
  • Disable AI features (stop using them anytime)

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Voaice is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. IN-APP PURCHASES

  • We offer a one-time purchase to remove advertisements
  • Payment is processed by Google Play Store
  • We do not store your payment information
  • Purchases are non-refundable as per Google Play policy

9. ADVERTISEMENTS

  • We display ads via Google AdMob in the free version
  • Ads may be personalized based on your interests
  • You can remove ads by purchasing the premium version
  • You can opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings

10. DATA RETENTION

  • Notes and recordings: Stored until you delete them
  • App settings: Stored until you uninstall the app
  • Analytics data: Anonymized and retained for improvement purposes
  • Ad data: Managed by Google AdMob (see their policy)

11. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted:

  • In the app (Settings → Privacy Policy)
  • On our website
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. YOUR CONSENT

By using Voaice, you consent to:

  • This Privacy Policy
  • Collection and use of information as described
  • Third-party service integrations

13. INTERNATIONAL USERS

Voaice is available globally. By using the app outside India, you consent to the transfer of your data to India for processing.

14. GDPR COMPLIANCE (EU Users)

If you are in the European Union:

  • You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data
  • You can withdraw consent at any time
  • You can file a complaint with your data protection authority
  • Data processing is based on your consent and legitimate interests

15. CALIFORNIA PRIVACY RIGHTS (CCPA)

If you are a California resident:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell your data)
  • Right to non-discrimination

16. CONTACT US

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy:

  • Developer: Thosho Tech
  • Email: contact@thoshotech.com
  • Website: https://thoshotech.com

Response time: Within 48 hours

SUMMARY

  • ✓ Your data stays on YOUR device
  • ✓ We don't store your notes on servers
  • ✓ Ads are served by Google AdMob
  • ✓ AI features are optional and process data temporarily
  • ✓ You can delete all data by uninstalling the app
  • ✓ One-time payment removes all ads forever

By downloading and using Voaice, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
