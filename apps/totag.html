Totag : AI Bookmark Manager

Take control of your digital reading list with TOTAG ; the smartest way to save, read, and summarize website URLs.

Tired of cluttered bookmarks and endless "tabs for later"? TOTAG is a privacy-focused, local-first bookmark manager designed for power users. Whether you want to read articles in reader mode, listen to them on the go, or get instant AI insights, TOTAG makes it effortless.

AI-POWERED SUMMARIES

  • Don’t have time to read a long article? Get the "Too Long; Didn't Read" (TL;DR) in seconds.
  • Multi-Model Support: Choose between Gemini, Groq, or keep it 100% private with Engaine (Local AI).
  • Smart Insights: Get 3-point bullet summaries that capture the core message of any link.
  • Visual Magic: Once summarized, a chip Icon marks your bookmarks for quick reference.

DISTRACTION-FREE READER MODE

  • Experience the web without any distraction. Focus only on the content that matters.
  • Customization: Change font sizes (12sp to 36sp) and toggle between Light and Dark modes.
  • Seamless Fallback: For complex sites, switch to a standard WebView with a single tap.

LISTEN ON THE GO (TTS)

  • Turn any article into a personal podcast.
  • Native TTS: High-quality text-to-speech using your device’s engine.
  • Smart Chunking: Smooth, stutter-free playback even for long-form essays.

POWERFUL ORGANIZATION

  • Folders & #Tags: Categorize your links exactly how you think.
  • Clipboard Detection: Copy a link, open TOTAG, and watch it auto-detect for instant saving.
  • Bulk Actions: Long-press to move or delete multiple bookmarks at once.
  • Smart Search: Find any saved link instantly by Title, URL, or Tag.

PRIVACY & FREEDOM

  • Local-First: Your bookmarks are stored in a local Room database on your device and there is an option backup in your own Google drive.
  • Local AI: Use Engaine app to summarize articles without your data ever leaving your phone.
  • No Account Required: Start saving links immediately.
