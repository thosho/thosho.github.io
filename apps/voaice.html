Voaice : AI Voice Notes & TTS

Your All-in-One AI Voice & Text Studio App

Transform the way you capture ideas with Voaice. Whether you are a student, writer, or busy professional, Voaice bridges the gap between spoken words and polished content. Record live, transcribe existing files, and use advanced AI to refine your writing—all in one powerful native app.

Key Features

  • 🎙️ Live Voice Typing: Say goodbye to slow typing. Capture your thoughts in real-time with high-accuracy speech-to-text for instant note-taking.
  • 📂 Audio-to-Text Converter: Have an existing recording? Upload your audio files and get a precise, editable transcript in seconds.
  • 🤖 AI Proofreader & Editor: Take your text to the next level. Our built-in AI fixes grammar, summarizes long notes, and optimizes text to sound natural when read aloud.
  • 🔊 Natural Text-to-Speech (TTS): Convert any text into high-quality, human-like audio. Perfect for creating voiceovers or listening to your notes on the go.
  • ⏺️ Professional Sound Recorder: A dedicated tool for quick, high-fidelity audio capture whenever inspiration strikes.
  • 📤 Export & Share: Save your work as text or audio files and share them across your favorite platforms with a single tap.

Why Choose Voaice?

  • Productivity First: Move from a spoken idea to a polished, professional document faster than ever.
  • AI-Enhanced: Don’t just transcribe—improve. Use AI to summarize and format your notes so they are ready for emails, scripts, or reports.
  • Seamless Workflow: Record, Edit, and Convert back to audio without switching between multiple apps.
