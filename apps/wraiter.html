Wraiter : AI Writing Partner

Tired of writer's block? Struggling with grammar? Unleash your creativity with Wraiter, your personal AI-powered writing partner and smart editor designed to help you write better and faster, both online and offline.

Whether you're drafting an important email, writing an essay, or creating your next blog post, Wraiter provides the tools you need to refine your text.

KEY FEATURES

  • Advanced AI Writing Tools: Go beyond simple editing. Rewrite sentences, paraphrase paragraphs, shorten or expand text, and instantly change the tone and style of your writing to fit any audience.
  • Grammar & Spelling: Our powerful proofreader catches everything from simple typos to complex grammatical errors, ensuring your writing is always polished and professional.
  • Adaptable Style & Tone: Instantly transform your text. Make it more Creative, Narrative, or Humorous. Change the tone to be Intense, Candid, or even Sarcastic with a single tap.
  • Overcome Writer's Block: Stuck for ideas? Use the AI Suggest feature to generate sentences and concepts to keep your momentum going.
  • Offline & Private Mode: Your privacy is paramount. Download a local AI model and use Wraiter's features with no internet connection required. When you use the local model, your text and data never leave your device.
  • Universal API Support (BYO-API): Power users, rejoice! Wraiter allows you to connect to your own third-party AI service keys, including Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Perplexity, Anthropic and more, giving you complete control and flexibility.
  • Clean, Distraction-Free Editor: A simple and intuitive interface lets you focus on what matters most: your writing.
  • Local Document Library: Automatically save and organize all your documents right on your device for easy access anytime, anywhere.

WHO IS WRAITER FOR?

  • Students: Improve your essays, reports, and assignments with powerful proofreading and paraphrasing tools.
  • Professionals: Craft professional emails, documents, and presentations that make an impact.
  • Writers & Bloggers: Overcome writer's block, refine drafts, and explore creative new styles.
  • Language Learners: Improve your grammar, vocabulary, and sentence structure.
